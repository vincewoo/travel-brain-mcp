# Vertical slice test

Use these as natural-language requests after connecting the MCP server.

1. **Create a trip**
   - “Create a 4-day sample trip to Tokyo from October 12–15, 2026, using Asia/Tokyo timezone.”

2. **Research and save a place**
   - “Save Senso-ji as a place for this trip.”
   - “Record a research note that early morning is generally quieter, mark it semi-volatile, and attach the source I provide.”

3. **Plan it**
   - “Put Senso-ji on the itinerary at 8:00 AM on October 13 and make it flexible.”

4. **Experience it**
   - “We actually arrived at 8:22 and left at 9:15. Mark it visited; I’d rate it 5/5 and would return.”

5. **Journal it**
   - “Remember: coming early was absolutely worth it; the side streets were one of my favorite parts.”

6. **Recommend it**
   - “Mark Senso-ji as something I’d strongly recommend to friends, and tell them to arrive early.”

7. **Learn**
   - “Remember as a candidate preference that I like visiting popular sights early to avoid crowds.”

8. **Retrieve**
   - “Search my Travel Brain for things I’d recommend in Tokyo and explain which are firsthand versus only researched.”

Success criteria: the recommendation is backed by a visit, the journal note remains separate from the generated recommendation, and the preference is stored as candidate semantic memory.
